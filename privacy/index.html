Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Last Prepper is a mobile game made by an independent developer. This page explains what data the game uses, why, and how to delete it. If you have any question, write to privacy@lastprepper.games.

What stays on your device

Your games, your settings and your progress are saved on your phone or tablet. We never ask for your email, a password or any other personal details.

Your account on our server

When the game connects to the internet, our server creates an anonymous account for you, tied to a random identifier of your device. If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the account is also linked to that identity, so you can get it back on a new device. On the server we keep only what the game needs:

  • your player name (the one you choose) and your friend code;
  • your gold balance and the record of gold earned and spent;
  • your season pass progress and your daily tasks;
  • your friends and friend requests;
  • a snapshot of your bunker (rooms, people, buildings, biome and level) so your friends can visit it;
  • the gifts, notes and likes between friends. Notes are ready-made phrases: there is no chat and no free text;
  • your points in the weekly ranking;
  • the reports you make about other players.

Your name, your level, the snapshot of your bunker and your ranking points can be seen by your friends, by players who search for your name to add you, and in the rankings.

Purchases

Gold and the starter pack are bought through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. To deliver your gold, our server checks the purchase receipt with Apple or Google and keeps a record of it: the product, its price, the store, the date and your account.

Cloud save

If you turn it on in Settings, your games are copied to iCloud (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), under your account there. We do not receive or read that copy.

Achievements

If you are signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games, the achievements you unlock are saved to your account on that platform.

Game statistics

To see how the game is doing, our server records the days you play, your platform, the game version, the language and the country of your store, and how you progress through the game: the tutorial steps, your levels, your first expedition and catastrophe, habits, daily tasks, and when your sessions start and end. None of this includes your name or contact details. Some notices, such as a purchase or a new player with the name of their bunker, reach a private channel of the team.

Server time and notifications

The game checks the time over the internet so that its timers stay right even if the phone clock changes; that check sends nothing about you. Notifications about finished works, catastrophes and the merchant are scheduled by your own phone, not by a server, and can be turned off in Settings.

What we do not do

The game has no ads and no advertising tracking, and we do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing.

Where your data is

Our server is hosted by DigitalOcean in the United States. Apple and Google process purchases, Game Center, Google Play Games and cloud saves under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

What is on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. What is on our server is kept while you use the game. You can delete your account and all its data at any time from Settings → Delete my account and data, or by writing to privacy@lastprepper.games with your friend code (it starts with BUNK-); we do it within 30 days. Purchase records are kept for accounting and to prevent fraud, and copies of the database are kept for up to 90 days. All the details are on the delete your account page.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we have about you, and ask us to correct it, export it or delete it, by writing to privacy@lastprepper.games.

Children

We ask nobody for personal details, children included. There is no chat or free text between players, and player names are checked against a list of words that are not allowed.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its date.
