Delete your account and data

You can delete your Last Prepper account and all its data at any time.

From the game

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and data.
  3. Confirm twice. You need an internet connection.

Without the game

If you cannot open the game, write to privacy@lastprepper.games from any address with your friend code (it starts with BUNK-) and we will delete everything within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account on our server, with your player name and friend code; your friends and friend requests; the snapshot of your bunker that friends visit; the gifts, notes and likes; your gold, your pass and your ranking points; and the games saved on that phone. It cannot be undone.

What we keep

Purchase records (product, price, store and date) are kept for accounting and to prevent fraud. Copies of the database are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. If you turned on Cloud save, delete the copy in iCloud or Google Play Games from your phone’s settings.
