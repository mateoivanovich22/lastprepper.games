Questions

Is Last Prepper free?

Yes. It is free to play and has no ads. You can buy gold to finish works instantly or get decorations, but you never have to.

Does my bunker keep going when I close the game?

Yes. Works, crops and your people’s needs keep running in real time. When you come back, everything that happened while you were away is waiting for you.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Add friends with their code, visit their bunkers, leave them a gift box every day, send notes and likes, and compare your bunkers in the weekly ranking.

I am changing phones. Will I lose my bunker?

Turn on Cloud save in Settings to keep a copy of your bunker in iCloud or Google Play Games. If you bought something, Restore purchases in the shop brings the starter pack back.

What languages is it in?

English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. You can change it any time from the title screen or Settings.

How do I delete my account?

From Settings → Delete my account and data, inside the game. Everything about it is on the delete your account page.
