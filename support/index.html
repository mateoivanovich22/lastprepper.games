Support

Something is not working, or you have an idea? Write to us. We read every message.

Write to us

support@lastprepper.games

To help you faster, include:

  • your friend code (in Friends → My code, it starts with BUNK-);
  • your phone or tablet model;
  • what happened and, if you can, a screenshot.

Common questions

I bought gold and it did not arrive

The game confirms each purchase with the store and keeps trying by itself if the store is slow, so give it a few minutes with internet. If it still has not arrived after a few hours, write to us with your friend code and the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt.

I changed phones

If Cloud save was on, your bunker comes back on the new phone when you use the same iCloud or Google Play Games account. In the shop, Restore purchases brings back the starter pack.

Someone is bothering me

Open their card from Friends and use Report, or remove or block them. Notes between players are ready-made phrases: there is no chat.

I want to delete my account

You can do it in the game, from Settings. Everything about it is on the delete your account page.
